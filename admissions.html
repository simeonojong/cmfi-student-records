WHAT TO PREPARE

Helpful information for an enquiry

Having these details ready helps the school office guide you quickly. The office will confirm which documents are required for your child’s year group.

  • Student detailsFull name, date of birth, current class and a parent or guardian contact.
  • Academic historyThe most recent report or transcript, plus any transfer information where applicable.
  • Identity documentsA copy of the relevant identification and a recent passport photograph, if requested.
  • Learning needsTell the school about language support, health considerations or other needs early.
