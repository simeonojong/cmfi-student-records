THE STUDENT LEARNING HUB

Resources for better learning.

Practical guides for your school day, independent study and WASSCE preparation. Open a guide and start with one step today.

01Plan your study weekMake time for learning, rest and review.+
  1. List the subjects and topics you need to review.
  2. Break a large topic into small tasks you can finish in one study session.
  3. Choose a realistic time for each task and include short breaks.
  4. End the week by checking what you can explain without your notes.
Try today: write down three learning goals for this week.
02Read, reflect & writeMove from remembering to understanding.+
  1. Preview the title and headings before reading.
  2. After a short section, close the text and summarize the main idea.
  3. Choose evidence or an example that supports that idea.
  4. In your writing, use a clear point, supporting evidence and an explanation.
Try today: summarize one paragraph in your own words.
03Practise maths & scienceLearn from the steps, including mistakes.+
  1. Write down what the question gives you and what you need to find.
  2. Identify the principle, formula or method you will use.
  3. Show each step and include units where needed.
  4. Check your result, then note the reason for any mistake.
Try today: solve one problem again without looking at the worked example.
04Prepare for assessmentsPractise thoughtfully. Ask questions early.+
  1. Confirm your assessment topics and instructions with your teacher.
  2. Practise relevant questions and review the feedback.
  3. Use a timer for some practice sessions and read every instruction carefully.
  4. Ask a teacher about topics you do not yet understand.
Try today: make a short list of questions to ask in your next class.

These are general study guides. Follow your teachers’ guidance and the school’s current assessment instructions.
